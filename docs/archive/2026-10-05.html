CVE & Threat Analysis

moderate

On October 5, 2026, several critical vulnerabilities are under active exploitation. Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway products are particularly affected, with CVE-2026-88779 (CVSS 8.7) being a high-severity memory overflow vulnerability leading to denial-of-service, and CISA adding it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) Catalog. Additionally, CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772 are critical zero-day flaws allowing remote code execution that are actively exploited globally. Cisco has also issued an alert for CVE-2026-76504, a critical (CVSS 9.8) vulnerability in Catalyst SD-WAN Manager, allowing unauthenticated remote attackers to gain administrator access, with active exploitation confirmed and no fixes yet available.

Active threat intelligence for October 5, 2026, highlights several campaigns and malware. The ClingSTUN Linux backdoor is exploiting approximately two dozen known, unpatched vulnerabilities in internet-facing IoT devices from vendors like Ivanti, D-Link, and TP-Link, turning compromised systems into proxy nodes. Ransomware activity continues to be significant, with attacks observed against South Africa's air navigation provider and the University of Illinois Chicago. A China-nexus espionage campaign, UAT-11587, is targeting government and policy organizations in Asia, deploying the Antino backdoor through spear-phishing.
