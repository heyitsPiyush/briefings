Bug Bounty

deep

September 2026 has seen a surge in critical vulnerability disclosures, notably with Microsoft's Patch Tuesday addressing a record 972 CVEs, including two actively exploited zero-days. These include CVE-2026-85880, an elevation of privilege in Windows ALPC with a CVSS score of 7.8, and CVE-2026-81963, another elevation of privilege in the Windows Update Stack, also rated 7.8. Adobe also released an out-of-band advisory for Adobe Commerce, CVE-2026-75650, a CVSS 10.0 template-engine injection actively exploited in the wild. Google Pixel devices received an update for CVE-2026-58704, a high-severity privilege escalation in the Cellular Modem with a CVSS score of 8.0, under limited, targeted exploitation. Furthermore, critical remote code execution vulnerabilities were found in The Events Calendar WordPress plugin (CVE-2026-78006, CVSS 9.8) by Wordfence Argus, and an unauthenticated administrative access flaw in JFrog Artifactory (CVE-2026-82329, CVSS 9.8).

In bug bounty program news, Microsoft expanded its Dynamics 365 and Power Platform bounties up to $60,000 for cross-tenant vulnerabilities. HackerOne updated its platform to clarify hacker profile fields and improve Spot Check deadline displays. Intigriti was announced as the new provider for Adobe's Bug Bounty Program, effective September 1, 2026, and also launched "CrowdRecon" to capture reconnaissance data from trusted hackers. A notable payout involved a $1 million Vercel sandbox challenge which led to the discovery of critical Linux kernel flaws, resulting in approximately $325,000 in committed payouts, with findings submitted by AI-assisted researchers. Bugcrowd's CEO, Dave Gerry, highlighted a new trend where AI agents themselves are becoming targets for hacking. Researchers are also leveraging AI for bug bounty hunting, with resources emerging on optimizing tools like Claude Code for enhanced efficiency.
